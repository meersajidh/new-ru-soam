## Origin observation

The claim this product was founded on:

> Current practice management software focuses primarily on administration. AI tools
> focus primarily on documentation. Neither truly understands how therapists think
> and work.

Ru-Soam is our attempt addressing this misalignment, reflecting "how therapists think and work" concretely

## Vision statement

Practice doesn't run on transactions or documents — it runs on a **relationship**

> Practitioner ↔ Client — oriented around goals of healing and transformation, progressed iteratively, session by session.

Tools that don't understand this shape make the practitioner work for the record.

Ru-Soam enters as the **instrument of representation** of that relationship.

> Local, privacy-first clinical workbench, designed by therapists, for therapists

Plans, sessions, measurements, agreements — the representation moves **in step** with
the practice, staying current with the reality it influences, interaction by
interaction.

The vision is for that instrument to be so trustworthy, secure, intuitive and perceptive, that the relationship gets practitioner's
whole attention.

## Who it is for

The **solo mental-health practitioner in India**: psychologists, counselors,
psychiatrists running independent practices, usually on a duct-taped stack of calendar +
WhatsApp + spreadsheets + paper, or on products that have no migration story, no
clinical depth, and no sovereignty posture.

## The Goals

### Facets of the Relationship

| Facets               | In one line                                                      |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clinical**         | formulation, treatment goals, interventions, outcome measurement |
| **Safety**           | privacy, risk-screening, monitoring, crisis/safety planning      |
| **Legal/Compliance** | consent, MHA-2017 NR/AD, confidentiality limits, retention       |
| **Financial**        | fee terms, sliding scale, payments, receipts                     |
| **Operational**      | session-plan execution, scheduling, availability, logistics      |

The facets are invariant across case types and practitioner types.

### The Five Guarantees

All five are representation-qualities — the confirmation that
instrument-of-representation is the right frame:

| Guarantee       | To the practitioner                                      | As a representation-quality                        |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fidelity**    | "What it shows me is true, complete, current."           | the representation is true — its defining quality  |
| **Ease**        | "Feeding it costs minutes, not my evenings."             | the representation is easy to capture              |
| **Reliability** | "Never fails me in-session; never loses what I gave it." | the representation is always available, never lost |
| **Insight**     | "Shows me what needs attention before I ask."            | the representation is active                       |
| **Custody**     | "My client's privacy is honored, PHI in my safekeeping"  | the representation is safe and secured             |

Core Principle: **Local-first is not an implementation preference; it is the structural answer to privacy and sovereignty**.

## Differentiation

- **Sovereignty** — the only tool in the segment where client PHI stays with the
  practitioner, structurally (local-first, encrypted at rest, no plaintext cloud).
- **A real migration story** — India tools assume fresh starts; global tools sell
  data-entry services we structurally refuse. Bulk calendar→caseload migration +
  document attachment + enrichment loops instead.
- **Clinically-sound content out of the box** — India/MHA-aware template library
  (biopsychosocial intake, consent artifacts, screeners) where competitors hand you a
  blank form builder.
- **A mirror that talks back** — goal-lens views (attention, gaps, trends, the 360)
  built on the loop model, not bolted-on dashboards.

## Consequences for product shape

Navigation follows the practitioner's **recurring questions**, not entity types —
the derived Activity direction:

- _Today_ ("what needs me now?"),
- _Intake_ ("who's at my door?"),
- _Caseload_ ("where does this case stand?"),
- _Encounters_ ("run the session, capture as I go"),
- _Plans_ ("what did we agree?"),
- _Payments_ ("am I getting paid?"),
- _Library_ ("my reusable content"),
- _Trust_ ("is the instrument holding its guarantees?").
