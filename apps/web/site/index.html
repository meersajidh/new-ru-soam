Ru-Soam

A bridge between you and your client.

Named for the Lepcha cane suspension bridges of Sikkim — Ru (cane) + Soam (bridge). Each bridge is woven by hand from natural fibres and holds tension across impossible distances.

Ru-Soam carries the same idea for clinical practice: a careful structure, suspended quietly between you and your client. Nothing is structural alone; every fibre is doing its small job.
