First-run, in twelve seconds

  1. Launch the app. Choose a workspace name — anything ("primary", "locum").
  2. Set a passphrase. At least twelve characters; the strength meter will tell you.
  3. Write down the twelve recovery words on paper. These are the only way back in if you forget the passphrase.
  4. Press Finish setup. The workbench opens, empty, ready.
